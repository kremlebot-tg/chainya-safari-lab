Why we do this

Tea is not only a strict ceremony. It is also a reason to experiment

At Chaynya you can take your time and work out how one tea differs from another. We can brew two side by side, compare aroma and taste, and help you choose the one you prefer.

So we do not lecture you on the canon and we do not ask for silence. Want it brewed in an unusual way? We will brew it that way. Want the Chaozhou ceremony, where the pot is packed full and the tea drunk from tiny bowls? Say so and we will run it.

How supply works

01

Tasting

We come to you with a set matched to your format and brew it on the spot. Free, takes about an hour.

02

Selection

We build a menu around your kitchen and prices, and cost out a cup and a pot so you know your margin.

03

Supply

Deliveries on a schedule. If a tea runs out early, we bring more separately.
